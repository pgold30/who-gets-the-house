# Bounded exploratory extension, 22 September 2026

Written after inspecting source coverage and before fitting the inventory associations. This is an internal analysis plan, not a registered preregistration.

Use only StreetEasy total sales inventory, Single Family category, borough-month. Coverage is complete and positive for all four relevant boroughs over required prior months. Do not compare a battery of indicators. Use log base 2 of previous-calendar-month inventory at both sale dates, centered within borough on the unweighted monthly mean log inventory over December 2015-November 2025. A unit is a doubling relative to the borough reference. Publication dates of historical releases are unavailable: prior month does not guarantee the information was publicly available by the sale date. This is a retrospectively revised series, not a real-time vintage.

Keep the full 6,006-pair house sample if both endpoints match; otherwise report the common supported sample and its baseline. Use the existing borough-quarter, ZIP-year and buyer/seller controls, unrestricted cash-to-finance and finance-to-cash indicators, uninteracted inventory at both endpoints, and two endpoint financing-inventory interactions. Add endpoint financing-by-borough nuisance slopes (Brooklyn omitted) to separate fixed geographic differences in financing associations from local inventory movements. Report both without and with separate endpoint financing-specific linear calendar trends. Do not interpret stock inventory as an exogenous liquidity shock or as a direct measure of execution risk; StreetEasy single-family coverage differs from the administrative one-to-three-family sample.

Correct the existing credit extension by explicitly controlling uninteracted weekly rates at both endpoints. Quarterly effects do not absorb all weekly variation. Keep the two endpoint interactions, report the same calendar-trend sensitivity and preserve old results as historical comparisons.

For both exercises report parcel CR0 uncertainty and additional score-covariance sensitivity allowing dependence when pairs share either endpoint calendar quarter (regardless of endpoint role) or a parcel, with inclusion-exclusion to avoid duplicate counting. Also show an endpoint-year version; only ten years are available. Negative covariance eigenvalues, if any, are replaced by zero and disclosed. Use t/F reference distributions based on the number of calendar nodes minus one; these are approximate exploratory diagnostics, not exact small-cluster inference. Report both endpoint slopes, their joint zero test and the common-slope restriction (sum zero). Dependence between non-overlapping calendar periods is not generally covered. Keep the main gap estimator and its original bootstrap unchanged.
