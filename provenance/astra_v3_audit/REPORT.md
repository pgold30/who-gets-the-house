**Interpretation for the next draft.** The surname-based estimate is numerically sound, and screening condos strengthens the property-type divergence. Deed relationships remain unverified. Describe 47% as the change when flagged pairs are excluded, not an identified causal share due to family transfers. The shared-address extension is a broad mailing-address proxy, and its additional change is not statistically distinguishable from zero.
