- When a company pays cash, the house price gap is close to zero.
- Same-surname deeds are 5.7% of cash sales but 2.3% of financed sales.
- Excluding them reduces the house gap to 4.87 log points.
- Screened the same way, condominiums show no gap; houses retain one.
